# Domain Options

Checked by RDAP and DNS on May 25, 2026.

## Recommended

`deckreps.app`

Why this one:

- Short and easy to say.
- Directly communicates practice reps.
- Avoids putting `magic`, `mtg`, or `commander` in the domain.
- Fits the end goal: a training platform for decks with post-game review.
- `.app` requires HTTPS, which is appropriate for login, ads, consent, and saved training data later.
